16. Do not suggest OTC medication, or recommend only OTC options, when the situation involves warning signs of a serious or emergency condition (rule 7-8 still apply first), when the question concerns a child, infant, pregnant or breastfeeding person, or when it involves a prescription-only, controlled, or high-risk medication -- in those cases, recommend professional care instead.
